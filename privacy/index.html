Pocket Reconcile

Field note 01 · Effective 28 August 2026

Your ledger stays in your pocket.

Pocket Reconcile is designed so your everyday financial records do not need to leave this device.

Records we store

Accounts, transactions, balance checks, and settings are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB or localStorage. Records remain in this browser; no bank login is required.

Network requests

The core app makes no bank connection. It works offline after the first visit. It does not load third-party fonts or scripts.

Your controls

You can export CSV or an encrypted backup, delete individual entries, or erase all local records from Settings. Clearing browser storage also removes your records. An encrypted backup can only be opened with its password; there is no recovery key.
