Pocket Reconcile

Field note 01 · Effective 28 August 2026

Your ledger stays in your pocket.

Pocket Reconcile is designed so your everyday financial records do not need to leave this device.

Records we store

Accounts, transactions, balance checks, settings, and any license token are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB or localStorage. We cannot see, recover, or sync them.

Network requests

The core app works offline and contains no analytics, advertising, bank connections, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. If you buy or restore Field Kit, the app contacts Sociobot’s billing API only to open checkout or verify the license token. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and processes payment information; Pocket Reconcile never receives card details.

Your controls

You can export CSV or an encrypted backup, delete individual entries, or erase all local records from Settings. Clearing browser storage also removes your records. An encrypted backup can only be opened with its password; there is no recovery key.

Questions

Email privacy@sociobot.in. Also read the terms.
